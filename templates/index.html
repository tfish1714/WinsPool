    How the draft works

    Draft flow

    Players pick in the order shown in the pick queue. Click a team in the grid to preview it, then hit Confirm Pick to lock it in — nothing is drafted on a single click. Once your pick lands, the board and pick queue update instantly for everyone; no refresh needed.

    The clock card shows how long the current picker has been thinking, with the label shifting (On the clock → Taking it in… → getting restless → holding up the draft) the longer it runs.

    Chat

    The chat panel in the bottom-right is shared and live for the whole room — everyone in the draft sees every message. Click the header to collapse it; a badge shows how many messages you've missed while collapsed.

    Chat icons

    🏈 posts the current list of still-available teams into chat, so you don't have to scroll the grid to check. − / + collapses and expands the chat panel.
